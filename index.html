此时此刻

This very moment

Why this philosophy matters now

未来十年，有一件事高于事业与金钱：你有一个很小的女儿。

Over the coming decade, one thing ranks above career and money: you have a very young daughter.

这意味着你人生这一阶段有个特别之处：她的童年，与你职业的后半程，正同时发生。这也是为什么你的「优化函数」应该现在就改变——不是某个遥远的将来。

This makes this phase of your life unusual: her childhood and your later career years are happening simultaneously. That is why your optimisation function should change now — not at some distant point.

  • 事业机会会再来。
  • Career opportunities recur.
  • 投资机会会再来。
  • Investment opportunities recur.
  • 项目会再来。
  • Projects recur.
  • 孩子两岁、五岁、八岁、十二岁——不会再来。
  • A child being 2, 5, 8, 12 does not recur.

有些机会会再来。有些时间不会。你不需要同时把每个维度最大化。人生有季节：

Some opportunities return. Some time does not. You don't have to maximise every dimension simultaneously. There are seasons:

春生，夏长，秋收，冬藏
Spring grows, summer extends, autumn harvests, winter stores.
有的时期用于建造，有的用于收获，有的用于守护，有的用于在场。
Some periods are for building, some for harvesting, some for protecting, some for being present.

智慧的人生，不在每个季节都要求最大产出——它先认出现在是什么季节。接下来的每一章，都在回答同一件事：这个季节，该怎么活。

A wise life does not demand maximum output in every season — it first recognises which season it is. Every chapter that follows answers one question: how to live in this season.

静

Stillness

The root — from which the seven practices grow

静不是一章，也不是打坐。它是根——其余一切修习都从它长出。静让你能够知道：该减去什么、何时不干预、何时弯曲、哪场仗不打、条件何时反转、什么是够、何时离场。

Stillness is not a chapter, nor is it meditation. It is the root — everything else grows from it. Stillness is what allows you to know: what to remove, when not to intervene, when to bend, which battle not to fight, when conditions are reversing, what is enough, when to leave the stage.

前半生，加法是合理的——更多的知识、经验、金钱、关系、责任与成就。但总有一天，经济账反转了。人生的下一程，不是衰败，而是转变。

In the first half of life, addition makes sense — more knowledge, more experience, more money, more relationships, more responsibility, more accomplishment. But one day, the economics reverse. The next stage is not decline; it is transition.

不要问「还能加什么」，要问「什么重要到值得保留」。碎片化的信息与无尽的机会，带来的不是智慧而是困惑——更少的目标，产生更大的专注。

Don't ask "what else can I add?" Ask "what is important enough to keep?" Fragmented information and endless opportunities bring confusion rather than wisdom — fewer objectives create greater focus.

在你这个阶段，最稀缺的已不是金钱与机会，而是——

At your stage, the scarcest resources are no longer money or opportunity. They are:

领导

Leadership

用 · 我的实践方向 → 原则 → 能人 → 边界 → 反馈 → 让系统自己运转。
用 · my practiceDirection → principles → capable people → boundaries → feedback → let the system work.

治大国若烹小鲜——反复翻动小鱼，鱼就碎了。定好条件，停止不断干预。你的本能是结构化、治理、分析、把一切做得可防守——这些是财富，但它们的阴影是过度工程。有时更优的架构是：足够的结构让系统运转，然后放手。

Governing a great state is like cooking a small fish — turn it constantly and it falls apart. Establish the right conditions and stop interfering. Your instinct to structure, govern, analyse and make things defensible is valuable, but its shadow is over-engineering. Sometimes the superior architecture is: enough structure for the system to work, then leave it alone.

投资

Investing

你控制不了市场、利率、衰退、战争、估值、别人。你能控制：买什么、付什么价、分散度、流动性、仓位、质量、耐心、以及是否恐慌。

You cannot control markets, rates, recessions, wars, valuations, or other investors. You can control: what you buy, the price you pay, diversification, liquidity, position size, quality, patience — and whether you panic.

用 · my practiceBuild a portfolio that does not require you to predict the world correctly.

这即是道家式的「反脆弱」。

That is a Daoist version of antifragility.

家庭

Family

你无法制造孩子最终成为的那个人。你能创造：爱、安全、榜样、边界、见识、价值观、机会——然后让生命自行展开。

You cannot manufacture the person your child will eventually become. You can create: love, security, examples, boundaries, exposure, values, opportunities — then allow life to unfold.

Yourself

Don't constantly "fix" yourself. Build environments where desirable behaviour becomes natural. That is wu wei.

Cycles, optionality, humility

成功里藏着失败的种子；失败里有时藏着成功的种子。强制造脆弱；弱促成适应；稀缺生纪律；丰裕生懈怠。

Enough — define "enough"

Freedom ≈ Resources ÷ Wants
道 · 原理Dao · principle
知足者富，知止不殆
One who knows contentment is rich; one who knows when to stop is safe.
《道德经》第三十三、四十四章

大多数人把财富定义为「我拥有什么」。但整页哲学真正关心的不是财富——是自由。

Most people define wealth as what I possess. But this whole philosophy is not really about wealth — it is about freedom.

Without an explicit "enough number," the game has no finish line. Suppose someone has reached financial security yet still sacrifices sleep, health, family time, peace and freedom to push a comfortable number higher —
economically, he grows richer; philosophically, he grows poorer.

Compassion — have strength, without hurting with it

With employees, family, and yourself

我有三宝，持而保之：一曰慈，二曰俭，三曰不敢为天下先。
I have three treasures, held and kept: the first is compassion, the second frugality, the third not daring to be first in the world.
《道德经》第六十七章。俭与不敢为天下先，前面已有很多；相对缺少的，是慈。
Dao De Jing, Chapter 67. Frugality and not-daring-to-be-first are already well covered above; what is relatively absent is compassion.

慈不是滥情，也不是软弱。它是：随着能力增长，变得更温和，而不是更硬。

Compassion is not sentimentality, nor weakness. It is: as capability increases, become gentler rather than harder.

  • 对下属——不羞辱。力量不需要靠伤害来显示。
  • With employees — don't humiliate. Strength needs no display through harm.
  • 对家人——不把人物化。家人不是要优化的项目。
  • With family — don't treat people as things. Family is not a project to be optimised.
  • 对自己——不把每个不完美都当作需要修正的缺陷。
  • With yourself — don't treat every imperfection as a defect requiring correction.
  • 对不如自己懂的人——不把知识当武器。
  • With those who know less — don't use knowledge as a weapon.

内在自由

Inner freedom

How much power does the external world have over your internal state?

你已经为「如何在外部世界立足」建立了相当强的哲学——事业、投资、选择权、第一性原理、系统思维、家庭。下一步的进化，可能不同：

You have already built a strong philosophy for how to navigate the external world — career, investing, optionality, first principles, systems thinking, family. The next evolution may be different:

不再是——「我如何优化我在世界中的位置？」
而是——「外部世界，对我内心状态能有多大权力？」
Not — "How can I optimise my position in the world?"
But — "How much power should the external world have over my internal state?"
  • 组合跌了 20%——你还是你吗？
  • If your portfolio falls 20%, are you still Thomas?
  • 别人拿到了你想要的位置——你还是你吗？
  • If someone gets a position you wanted, are you still Thomas?
  • 没人认可你的贡献——你还是你吗？
  • If people don't recognise your contribution, are you still Thomas?
  • 一个项目失败了——你还是你吗？
  • If a project fails, are you still Thomas?
  • 有人不同意你——你还是你吗？
  • If someone disagrees with you, are you still Thomas?

当答案越来越趋向「是」，你就得到了金钱与头衔买不到的东西：

When the answer becomes increasingly "yes," you acquire what money and title cannot buy:

自由
外界可以改变我的处境，
但不必决定我的内心。

得，不足以使我狂。
失，不足以使我乱。
誉，不足以使我增。
毁，不足以使我减。

我仍然是我。
The world can change my circumstances,
but it need not decide my mind.

Gain cannot make me giddy.
Loss cannot unsettle me.
Praise cannot add to me.
Blame cannot subtract from me.

I am still myself.
年轻时，我们靠得到东西建立自己。
到了下一程，我们靠「不再需要那么多东西」找回自己。
In youth, we build ourselves by acquiring.
In the next stage, we rediscover ourselves by needing less.
The first half of life is often about acquiring.
The second half is learning what you can release without becoming less.
